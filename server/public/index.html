CampusFallback

Canvas went down two hours before my deadline, and I was fine.

  1. A Chrome extension keeps an offline copy of your Canvas courses: assignments, announcements, syllabi, files and teacher contacts.
  2. If Canvas, NetID or Microsoft is down near a deadline, it seals your file's fingerprint with a trusted timestamp from FreeTSA and drafts an email to your professor.
  3. When Canvas comes back, it submits the file automatically, and anyone can check here that it is the same file that was sealed.
